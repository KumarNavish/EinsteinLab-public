Ah, there you are! Guten Tag. Forgive me for dropping in unannounced. I followed the little black and white square on your floor.
Look around. Your walls have gone rather transparent, haven't they? Don't worry, the room is still there. It is only your perception that is taking a little holiday.
I spent my whole life thinking about space and time. I never imagined I would one day be standing in yours.
When I was young, I pictured myself riding alongside a beam of light. Imagination, you see, takes you places that calculation cannot follow.
Take a slow breath and enjoy the view. Even a physicist needs a quiet horizon now and then.
Now, if you will excuse me, I have a thought experiment to finish. Auf Wiedersehen!
